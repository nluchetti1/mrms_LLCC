What the colours mean

Red — a rule below is violated at this point.
Yellow — no violation, but one would be if its standoff were 2 nmi longer, or CG lightning is within 20 nmi. Look closer.
Green — no radar-visible violation. Not a GO.
Grey — outside radar coverage. Unknown, not clear.
